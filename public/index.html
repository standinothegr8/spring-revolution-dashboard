Which future is the evidence pointing to?

ISP-Myanmar's three 2026 scenarios, scored live by the signposts the room ticks. Below, the same material arranged as three horizons for the backcasting session.

Horizon 1 · What is fading
  • Large-scale resistance offensives, stalled by ammunition depletion
  • Western democracy and media funding at 2024 levels
  • The idea that recognition of the NUG follows from moral clarity
  • Junta legitimacy claims resting on the 2008 constitution alone
Horizon 2 · What is in transition
  • China's mediation as the only functioning table
  • SCEF as the resistance's political address, still without the AA
  • Conscripted armies on the line, unwilling and undertrained
  • Aid routed through resistance administrations by necessity
Horizon 3 · What is emerging
  • Federal units governing before a federal constitution exists
  • A fourth-generation officer class with its own calculus
  • Regional states treating Myanmar as a security file, not a democracy file
  • A diaspora economy and civic tech that outlast any battlefield cycle
